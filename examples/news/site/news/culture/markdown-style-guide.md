Stories are written in Markdown. This page shows each element as readers will see it, so you can choose the right one for what you have to say.

## Paragraphs and emphasis

Most of a story is paragraphs. Use _italics_ for titles of works and for a word that needs stress, and **bold** sparingly, for the one thing a scanning reader must not miss. Link with a short phrase that says where the link goes: [the topics index](/tags), not "click here".

## Headings

Use `##` for the sections of a long story and `###` for parts of a section. The headline is the page's only top-level heading, so start at `##`.

### A third-level heading

It sits under a section, for a story with distinct parts.

## Quotes

> A quote set apart from the text gives a reader the words of someone in the story, in full.

Use one where the exact words matter. Shorter quotes belong in the paragraph, in quotation marks.

## Lists

Unordered lists, for items in no particular order:

- what happened;
- who it affects;
- what happens next.

Ordered lists, for steps or rankings:

1. Read the story aloud.
2. Cut what you stumble over.
3. Check every name and number.
