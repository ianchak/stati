Stati News is a placeholder newsroom. Replace this page with who you are, what you cover and how readers can reach you: a tip line, an email address for corrections, and your editorial standards.

## How this site is built

Every page is a Markdown file in `site/`, rendered by [Stati](https://stati.build). Articles live in a folder per section under `site/news/`, the sections and authors are listed once in `stati.config.js`, and the front page ranks the stories by their front matter. The sample articles explain each part.

## Corrections

Tell readers how to report a mistake and how you mark a corrected story. The article layout shows an "Updated" line when an article's front matter has an `updated` date.
