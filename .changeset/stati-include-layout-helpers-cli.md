The CLI runs `@stati/core` 2, in which templates compose with `stati.include()` and `stati.layout()` and the config's `eta.filters` becomes `helpers`. Upgrade `@stati/core` and `@stati/cli` together, as the [upgrade guide](https://stati.build/getting-started/upgrading-to-2) describes. Commands and flags are unchanged.
